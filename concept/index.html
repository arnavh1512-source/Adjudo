One claim, start to finish.

  1. Documents in
  2. Policy applied
  3. Decision signed
  4. Portal filled
  5. Query answered

  1. Documents in

    Policy, bill, discharge summary and pre-auth letter, as PDFs or photos.

  2. Policy applied

    The engine walks the bill against the policy, rule by rule. Same inputs, same number.

  3. Decision signed

    Approve, query or deny, written to a signed, hash-chained audit row.

  4. Portal filled

    The Copilot, a Chrome extension, types it into the insurer or TPA portal. You submit.

  5. Query answered

    When the insurer asks why, Adjudo drafts the reply with the clause that answers it.
