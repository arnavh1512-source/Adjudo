Pricing FAQ
›
Do you charge per claim or per seat?

Both, depending on tier. Pilot is a flat platform fee. Growth and Enterprise blend a platform fee with per-adjudication pricing that decreases with volume. Reviewer-console seats are bundled — we don't charge per officer.

›
How long is a typical pilot?

30–60 days. We onboard one line of business, run shadow mode for 1–2 weeks (decisions reviewed but not enforced), then move to assist or auto. We guarantee a written ROI report at the end.

›
Can we run on-prem?

Yes. Indian hospital networks bound by NABH and insurers with strict data residency rules can run Adjudo entirely inside their VPC, on a single GPU appliance, or fully air-gapped. The product is the same.

What about IRDAI, DPDP, NABH?

›
Do you train on our data?

No — never on customer claims, by default. If you opt in to a custom fine-tune, training runs in your tenant on your data only, and the resulting weights are yours.

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
Built for regulators, not just demo days.

Every Adjudo decision is encrypted, audited, and cryptographically signed — by construction, not by policy memo. Here's how.

Encryption everywhere

TLS 1.3 in transit. AES-256 at rest. Per-tenant encryption keys, rotated quarterly. KMS-managed (AWS KMS / HashiCorp Vault on-prem).

Signed by construction

Every adjudication writes an HMAC-SHA256-signed audit row, hash-chained to the previous row. Tampering breaks the chain. UPDATE / DELETE are revoked at the database role.

Cloud or on-prem

Hosted in AWS ap-south-1 (India). Single-tenant VPC available. NABH-bound hospitals can run a full air-gapped install on a single GPU appliance.

Least-privilege access

SSO via SAML / OIDC. Role-based access with row-level security on Postgres. All production access goes through short-lived credentials and bastion hosts. Customer patient data is never accessible to engineers without a logged JIT request.

Audited end-to-end

Every API call, every model inference, every reviewer decision is logged with prompt SHA-256 and model version. Logs retained for 8 years to meet IRDAI requirements.

No training on your claims

We never train on customer data without explicit opt-in. If you opt in to a custom fine-tune, training runs in your tenant on your data, and the weights stay yours.
