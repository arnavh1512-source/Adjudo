Frequently asked.

›
Is it IRDAI compliant?

We meet IRDAI's audit-trail expectations: append-only adjudication ledger, 8-year retention, signed and hash-chained, with full prompt + model version provenance.

›
Do you handle NABH and NPPA?

Yes. NABH tariffs are encoded as policy clauses; NPPA caps are enforced at the line-item level. Both are cited explicitly on every deduction.

›
What about Hindi-speaking officers?

The reviewer console supports English UI today; chat replies in English by default but the underlying model can produce Hindi when the officer asks.

›
Can we run on-prem?

Yes. NABH-bound hospitals and TPAs with strict data residency rules can run Adjudo entirely inside their VPC or on a single GPU appliance.
