The pain we hear, every week.

01

Cashless approval queues

Claims officers spend 12–18 minutes per cashless pre-auth on policy-wording lookup alone. Adjudo hands it back with the cited policy clauses, NABH tariff and NPPA caps already attached.

02

Sub-limit and NPPA leakage

Room-rent caps, proportionate deductions, disease sub-limits and NPPA price ceilings get missed at the desk — and the money leaks out. A constrained-decoded model applies every clause the same way, on every claim, each one cited.

03

IRDAI audit defensibility

When IRDAI, an Ombudsman, or a policyholder's counsel asks why — you need the cited policy wording, the prompt that produced the decision, and a tamper-evident signature. We give you all three on every adjudication.

04

Disputes with hospitals

When a network hospital pushes back on a deduction, your officer has minutes to defend it. Cited clauses plus NABH and NPPA references on every Adjudo decision means the answer is already written.

Frequently asked.

›
Is it IRDAI compliant?

We meet IRDAI's audit-trail expectations: an append-only adjudication ledger, 8-year retention, signed and hash-chained, with full prompt + model version provenance. ISO 27001-aligned; DPA available on Growth and Enterprise. Policyholder data stays inside your tenant.

›
Does it handle NABH and NPPA rules?

Yes. NABH tariffs are encoded as policy clauses, ICD-10 coding and bundling logic are modelled alongside your policy wordings, and NPPA price caps are enforced at the line-item level. The model is fine-tuned on real Indian adjudication patterns.

›
Do you integrate with our claims platform?

We've integrated with the major Indian health-claims and TPA platforms as well as home-grown systems. Claims in, decisions out, with the audit trail attached as a sibling artifact.

›
What happens when the model is uncertain?

Adjudo emits a calibrated confidence score per decision. Below your configurable threshold, the claim is routed to a human with the model's reasoning + cited clauses pre-loaded.
