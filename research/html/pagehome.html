How it works

From inbox to settled — end to end.

1

Drop the docs

Policy, hospital bill, discharge, pre-auth letter. PDFs or photos — the flow your reviewers already run.

2

Adjudo adjudicates

The engine reads the policy and walks the bill line-by-line, applying every rule deterministically — each field cited.

3

Signed decision out

Approve / query / deny with reasoning, cited clauses, and a tamper-evident audit row your compliance team will love.

4

Copilot fills the portal

The Copilot types the decision straight into the insurer / TPA portal (IHX & co.) — you review and submit, no re-keying.

5

Query? Reply, cited

When the insurer raises a query, Adjudo drafts the response with the exact clause and document that answers it — ready to send.
