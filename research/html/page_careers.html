How we work

The rules we won't break.

Small team. High bar. Direct feedback. The rules we won't break, even when shipping fast.

01
Real claims or it didn't happen

We train and test on the same kind of claim a TPA officer rejects on a Tuesday afternoon. Synthetic-only is theatre.

02
Cited or it didn't happen

Every decision references the clauses it relied on. No exceptions. The model loses points if it skips a citation, even when right.

03
Signed or it didn't happen

Every adjudication writes a signed, append-only audit row. Tampering breaks the chain. Compliance isn't a feature — it's the foundation.

04
Ship to the customer's environment

Some customers are cloud, some are on-prem behind hospital firewalls. We meet them where they are, with the same product.
