Healthcare workflows

Built for
India's payers.

Not a generic claims tool. Adjudo was trained on Indian policy schedules (IRDAI structure, NABH/non-NABH distinctions), hospital tariffs and SOC rate cards, and the actual deduction logic your reviewers apply.

01

Pre-authorization

  • Hospital submits pre-auth with policy + diagnosis + estimated cost
  • Adjudo matches to the policy schedule, applies tariff rates and sub-limits, returns approval / query
  • Returns a cited approval / query decision, not a queue ticket
02

Cashless · Final bill

  • On discharge, hospital pushes final bill (itemised) + discharge summary
  • Adjudo re-adjudicates against approved pre-auth + new line items
  • Flags scope creep, unbundling, room-rate overruns, tariff drift
03

Reimbursement

  • Member uploads bill + discharge + investigations on portal
  • Adjudo runs full adjudication: policy match, exclusions, sub-limits, co-payment, deductions
  • Outputs an itemised settlement letter ready for ops review
